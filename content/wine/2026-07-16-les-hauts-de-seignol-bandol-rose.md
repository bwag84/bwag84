## First impression

Fresh, but not compelling.

## What I tasted

An unpleasant bitter note and a flat, not especially complex flavour profile. The freshness was lovely, but not enough to make it enjoyable on its own.

## Verdict

Do not buy again for casual drinking. It may make more sense alongside oily fish, fried food or another rich dish.
