## First impression

Really enjoyable: smooth, generous and fruit-forward.

## What I tasted

An incredible, strong sweet-vanilla character alongside generous fruit. It is a lighter red, but the softness and flavour make it highly enjoyable.

## Verdict

80/100. I would buy it again when back home. It also shows that I can enjoy a lighter red when it has enough ripe fruit, softness and obvious vanilla character.
